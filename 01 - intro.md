# Background
Paseo before it even had a name started off as a way for me to organize myself to book travel for a vacation to anywhere.    
Using my **favorite travel apps**, I would search for best deals on flights, then once I nailed that down I would look for stays   
for my trip.   

But when under time pressure and with the variability of ***price changes***, ***availability*** and ***when to actually book travel***    
whether it's too early or too late, indecision started creeping into my travel planning. I tried all types of ways to combat this:    
<u>the classic spreadsheet</u> with all flights and stays listed to <u>a little script</u> that would contain (what I thought at the time) simple   
business logic for ranking flights and selecting one to an <u>in-browser script</u> to handle decision making on travel sites themselves     
to eventually incorporating <u>machine learning</u>, a data ingestion pipeline, a chat interface, and an agentic framework of services.


Sounds complicated, huh?     

Yeah it was too complicated and all that I described above, I never finished.
So it's back to square one and this time     

I'm going to build it the right smarter way.
