# Design
When you look at the ChatPage implementation you can see that I only got as far handling Stays. Why?    
If you read my intro, while i was thinking about how to decide on FAST parts of a trip, naturally the first    
step would be to look at flights. That turned out to be the easy part in my opinion. Why?   
When it comes to finding flights, a lot of travel sites or apps do a good job of allowing the user    
to navigate, filter down and select a pretty good flight for their vacation or rather travel.    
And personally, normally, I can quickly manually narrow down which flight or flights I prefer using the standard    
method of just searching a travel site myself and getting the flight I want.  

However when it comes to stays, I find a lot of sites that offer the ability to search for stays do a poor job   
of allowing users to do what they normally do on flight searches. There's a lack of filtering, there's no best or worst  
ranking of stays, and there's no personalization. There seems to be some sort of algorithm that does some sort of ranking  
on most stays search sites, but often times I find myself scrolling and checking each and every option listed, viewing   
the details of that stay, and lastly if need be sending a message to the owner/provider of that stay for more details. 

That's a lot of work!

And it gets worse, when you are looking across many stay search platforms where they're not all necessarily the same   
or complete with information.
