# Future consideration (but out of scope)
There are many more features that I would love Paseo to encompass but they may be too large in scope for this project, namely:
- The ability to create trips around a favorite past stay. For example if there was a stay in Villa de Leyva that I really   
  enjoyed and would like to go back to, it would be nice initiate a chat query beginning with planning a trip with that stay. 
  as the focus, finding it's dates of availability, then kind of going backwards and finding flights around those dates
- Finding multiple stays to span the entire trip. For example in Bogota, if there is a way to book split stays where part of the
  trip is spent at one stay versus another part of the trip is stayed at another stay in the same destination and this proves to 
  be a better deal than all or none at either stay (and maybe this split option could be the only one available because neither   
  stay is available for the entire start to end date of the trip), then this would be great
- Handling multiple destinations. This could mean a lot of things. It could be query that wants a trip planned around the inclusion of  
  multiple destination cities or it could mean finding the best trip at one of the destination cities and/or allow the user to compare 
  and choose for themselves which of the proposed trips they would prefer. There's a lot of ways to go with this.
- Handling multiple guests on one trip. For example you and a friend planning to go on a trip together but both are departing from  
  differnt cities, have different preferences, etc etc etc,.
- Then there's the simple (but not so simple) calendar integration whereby trips can be planned around your calendar free dates etc
- Taking an existing itinerary (could be generated by another trip app platform) and improving it. This couldt take the form of 
  searching for better stays, flights, etc if the user asks
- Being able to share a trip itinerary with someone and maybe actually allowing that person to either join your trip or duplicate your trip
- Having a record history of past trips to look back upon and maybe use to create future trips
- Creating tours that span several months or a year(s)
- Compariing trip options amongs different countries for different parts of the year
- Planning trips around events, conferences etc
- Having real-time updates either while on the trip or maybe just updates about parts of the trip that have been updated, so if a host responds to a message regarding a possible stay that you inquired about or if the price has changed up or down regarding a particular part of your FAST itinerary
